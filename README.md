## 📖 About

**SUDHIR TUTORIALS** is a modern educational platform designed to provide students with a complete digital learning experience. The platform enables students to access study materials, assignments, tests, notes, and performance tracking anytime, anywhere.

Our mission is to make quality education accessible, affordable, and effective for every learner.

---

## ✨ Features

### 👨‍🎓 Student Features

* 📚 Chapter-wise Notes
* 🎥 Video Lectures
* 📝 Assignments & Practice Sheets
* 📊 Online Test Series
* 📈 Performance Analytics
* 🔔 Real-time Notifications
* 📱 Mobile-Friendly Learning
* 🎯 Progress Tracking

### 👨‍🏫 Teacher Features

* Upload Study Materials
* Create Tests & Assignments
* Student Management
* Attendance Tracking
* Performance Monitoring
* Announcements & Updates
